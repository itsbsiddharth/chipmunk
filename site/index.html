The loop it kills

Your AI can't see your screen. So whenever it needs to, for a page that looks wrong, an error, or a design you love, it's screenshot, switch, paste, explain, switch back, again and again, while your prompt waits in a chat box that can reset. Then the AI mixes up which screenshot you meant. Coding tools are worse: on Windows, pasting several screenshots into Claude Code, Codex or Cursor often attaches only one, or scrambles the order. Chipmunk pastes every one, in order, with your notes, every time.

Without Chipmunk

  1. Screenshot
  2. Switch to the chat
  3. Paste
  4. Switch back
  5. Screenshot again
  6. Switch, paste, switch…
  7. Type the prompt in a tiny box
  8. Hope the tab doesn't reload

With Chipmunk

  1. Screenshot as usual. Each one lands in your draft, numbered.
  2. Say what matters: add a note to an image, or write "in image 2…" in your prompt.
  3. Click Send. Everything pastes in, in one go.

About 15 seconds instead of two minutes, and you never leave what you're doing.

Features

Small app. Big difference.

Numbered screenshots, with notes

Use Win+Shift+S like always. While you're working on a draft, each screenshot lands in it with a number. Add a note to any image, and the AI gets "Image 2: your note" in order.

One click to any AI

Send lists every open AI app and window. It switches there and pastes images, files and text together, then puts your clipboard back.

Never lose a prompt

Your draft saves on every keystroke, keeps a version every minute, and lives on your disk, not in a chat box that can reload.

Reuse past chats

Pull a prompt and its screenshots out of an earlier Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, ChatGPT or Claude chat into a new one.

Works with your dictation

Wispr Flow, Granola, Superwhisper, Aqua or Windows voice typing type straight into the draft. Talk through your prompt.

Built for coding agents

In a terminal, files go to Claude Code as @paths. Or connect over MCP and say "use my Chipmunk draft".

Meet Chip

A tiny helper that saves you trips.

Chip sits at the edge of your screen and stays quiet unless it can save you a trip. Click it to open your draft. Drop files, images or text on it to collect them. A badge counts what's in the draft, and a small glowing dot shows when Chipmunk is collecting every screenshot, so you always know. Chip never appears in your screenshots or screen recordings. It's optional: one switch turns it off.

Install

Running in under a minute.

  1. Download Chipmunk-Setup.exe.
  2. Open it. Chipmunk installs itself and opens. No admin rights, nothing to click through.
  3. Press Win+Alt+P or click Chip, take a screenshot, and hit Send.

Seeing "Windows protected your PC"? That's Windows being careful with new apps that aren't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. The code is open on GitHub.

Prefer no installer? Get the portable zip. Uninstall any time from Settings > Apps.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Chipmunk is free and open source under the MIT license. No account, no subscription.

Does it upload my screenshots anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your PC in Documents\Chipmunk. Your screenshots only go to the AI app you paste them into, the same as if you pasted them yourself. The only thing Chipmunk sends over the internet is a daily check for updates, which you can turn off.

Which AI apps does it work with?

Anything with a text box: ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini in the browser or their desktop apps, Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity in a terminal, Cursor, Copilot, even Slack. Chipmunk recognises the common AI apps and lists them first.

Will it grab my personal screenshots?

No. Screenshots go into the draft by themselves only while you're working on it: the panel is in front, or you used it in the last few minutes. Otherwise Chip asks "Add this screenshot to your draft?" and it takes one click. If you want everything, turn on Collect every screenshot. It shows a glowing dot, and it turns itself off when you send.

Does it take screenshots out of my Screenshots folder?

No. Windows still saves every screenshot to Pictures\Screenshots, as usual. Chipmunk only copies from the clipboard, so nothing is taken out of that folder.

How do I point the AI at one screenshot?

Every screenshot in your draft has a number, and a small "Add a note for the AI" field. When you send, the images paste first, in order, so the AI app calls them Image 1, 2, 3, and your notes go with them ("Image 2: the button is cut off"). You can also just write "in image 2…" in your prompt.

Do I have to use Chip? Will it clash with my Codex pet?

Chip is optional. One switch turns it off, and the welcome screen asks whether you want it. It never appears in your screenshots and doesn't take any shortcut a pet uses. Many people run Chipmunk with Chip off and just press Win+Alt+P.

Does it work with Wispr Flow?

Yes. Click in the prompt and use Wispr Flow, Granola, Superwhisper, Aqua or Windows voice typing (Win+H) as you normally would. Your words land in the draft.

Can I reuse chats from the ChatGPT app?

Yes. Export your data from ChatGPT (Settings > Data controls > Export) and import the .zip in Chipmunk's Past chats. Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity chats are read straight from your PC.

Mac?

Yes, as a beta. Download Chipmunk-mac.zip from the latest release, right-click the app and choose Open the first time. It lives in the menu bar, collects your ⌘⇧4 screenshots while you work on a draft and pastes everything into your AI app. Chip, Past chats and the browser extension are Windows-only for now.

How do I uninstall it?

Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Chipmunk > Uninstall. Your drafts stay in Documents\Chipmunk.
